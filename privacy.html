1. Who We Are

Yenepoya Specialty Hospital (a unit of Yenepoya Institute of Medical Sciences and Research Pvt Ltd), Mangalore, Karnataka, India — hereinafter referred to as "YSH", "we", or "the Hospital" — operates the InternPulse intern management system.

As the operator of this system, YSH is the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, 2023. You, as the intern registering on this platform, are the Data Principal.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the basis of your explicit, informed consent provided at the time of registration, as required under Section 6 of the DPDP Act, 2023. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting the Grievance Officer below.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To register and manage your internship at YSH
  • To assign a posting location and mentor
  • To verify your attendance during the posting period
  • To track your learning progress and assess your knowledge
  • To validate your report submission and issue your internship certificate
  • To maintain an archive of completed internships for institutional records
Your data is used only for internship management. It is not used for marketing, profiling, or shared with any third party outside of YSH.

5. Data Sharing

Your personal data is not sold, rented, or shared with any external party. Within YSH, access is limited to:

  • The Academic Coordinator / Director's Office (full access)
  • Your assigned Mentor (limited — name, college, objective, learning logs)

Data is stored on Supabase (Singapore region), a secured cloud database platform. Supabase processes data as a Data Processor on behalf of YSH.

6. Data Retention

In accordance with Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act, 2023, your personal data will be retained for a period of three (3) years from the date your internship is marked as completed. After this period, your data will be flagged for review and deleted unless a specific legal or institutional requirement mandates retention.

8. Data Security

YSH implements the following measures to protect your personal data:

  • Role-based access control — only authorised coordinators access the admin dashboard
  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) for all data in transit
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies govern database access
  • Photographs stored in a secured, access-controlled storage bucket

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, YSH will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals as required under Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act, 2023.

9. Photograph — Special Notice

Your photograph is collected solely for identity verification within the hospital premises — to distinguish interns from patients and visitors. It is stored securely and displayed only within the InternPulse system to authorised staff. It is not published publicly or shared externally.
